# Ask Formats

> The three shapes written most often to the owner, each in one home (rule:collaborative-design-locks has the A/B/C shape these serve). Plain words (rule:owners-rules #8).

## Decision pop-up

The choice pop-up the owner taps (AskUserQuestion), one decision per question:

- **Question**, about twelve words, standing alone: what is decided and the fact that raised it.
- **Two to four options**, each a label of one to five words and one or two sentences on its outcome and cost.
- **One option recommended:** listed first, its label ending "(Recommended)".
- **Why and proof** in the recommended option's sentences: the measurement, quoted line or run behind it.
- A yes for an outward act quotes the act exactly in one code span, with one option starting "Yes" and one "No" (rule:outward-acts).

## 60-second overview

Three lines, before any detail or item code:

1. **What we are looking at:** <the part, in plain words>.
2. **What was found or measured:** <the fact, with its number or source>.
3. **What is being decided:** <the choice, or "nothing: for your information">.
