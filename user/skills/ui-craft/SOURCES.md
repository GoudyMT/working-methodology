Every idea was rewritten in the kit's words; no source text was copied beyond a short phrase. Paths in our files
are under `references/` unless shown as `SKILL.md`.

web-animation-design state they are based on Emil Kowalski's design engineering practice and his "Animations on
the Web" course; its web-design-guidelines names Vercel as author. vectorlab-ux-skills (MIT, VectorLab) cites
Jon Yablonski's Laws of UX. design (Anthropic) and frontend-design (Anthropic) are Apache-2.0.
